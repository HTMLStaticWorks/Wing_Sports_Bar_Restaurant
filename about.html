BUILT FOR BIG GAMES & BIGGER NIGHTS.

OUR STORY

A RESTAURANT CREATED AROUND THE IDEA THAT GREAT WINGS AND LIVE SPORTS BELONG TOGETHER.

We didn't just want another sports bar. We wanted a premium experience where the food was as exciting as the game itself.

2015

The First Wing

It started in a small test kitchen. We spent six months perfecting the double-fry technique to ensure our wings stayed shatter-crisp even when drenched in sauce.

2016

The Signature Sauce

After 100+ iterations, our Classic Buffalo and Garlic Parmesan sauces were born. We refused to use bottled shortcuts.

2018

The First Game Night

We opened our doors on championship Sunday. We installed 40 screens, set up the surround sound, and poured the first pint. The energy was electric.

2022

The Community

We became the official headquarters for local fan clubs, fantasy leagues, and victory celebrations.

Today

Never Ordinary

With multiple locations and an ever-evolving menu, we continue to deliver the premium sports-bar experience you deserve.

Our Philosophy

Flavor First

No frozen shortcuts. Everything from our sauces to our rubs is crafted in-house to ensure maximum impact.

Game Always On

If it's a big game, we're showing it. Period. With state-of-the-art AV, there isn't a bad seat in the house.

Bring Everyone Together

Our layouts, our shared platters, and our atmosphere are designed for groups to celebrate together.

Never Ordinary

We elevated the sports bar. Premium service, dramatic lighting, and a modern energetic vibe.

More Than A Bar. A Community.

From local sports nights to charity game events, we're proud to be the gathering place for passionate fans.
