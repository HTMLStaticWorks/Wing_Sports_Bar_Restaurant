The Perfect Wing.

Our commitment to doing it right, every single time.

01

Season & Prep

We start with fresh, never frozen jumbo wings. Hand-rubbed with our secret spice blend and rested for 24 hours to lock in maximum flavor.

02

The Double Crisp

Fried twice. Once to cook through, and a flash fry right before serving to create that unmistakable shatter-crisp exterior.

03

The Sauce Toss

Transferred hot to a steel bowl and tossed vigorously in your choice of our 12 house-made signature sauces until perfectly coated.

04

Serve Hot

Delivered to your table steaming hot, right as the whistle blows. Served with house-made ranch, bleu cheese, and crisp celery.
