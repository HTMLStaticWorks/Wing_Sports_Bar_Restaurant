Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept reservations for game days?
Yes! We highly recommend reserving your table in advance for major sporting events. You can use the form on this page or call us directly to secure premium seating.
Are children allowed in the sports bar?
We are fully family-friendly until 8:00 PM. After 8:00 PM, we transition to a 21+ environment to maintain the ultimate sports lounge experience for our adult guests.
Do you show pay-per-view UFC and boxing matches?
Absolutely. We show all major UFC PPV events and select high-profile boxing matches on our massive main screens. No cover charge applies if you arrive before 8 PM.
Can you accommodate large parties or private events?
Yes, we have private VIP sections and can accommodate large groups for birthdays, corporate events, and fantasy drafts. Please contact us for specialized group packages.
Do you have vegetarian or vegan wing options?
Yes, we offer premium cauliflower "wings" and plant-based tenders that can be tossed in any of our 12 signature sauces. We also offer dedicated vegan dips.
